HTML Lists

HTML lists are used to display groups of related items. Lists are useful for menus, instructions, features, categories and many other types of information.

Unordered Lists

An unordered list uses the <ul> element. Each item is placed inside an <li> element. Items are normally displayed with bullet points.

Why Use Lists?

  • They organize information clearly.
  • They make webpages easier to read.
  • They are useful for navigation menus.
  • They can represent steps and instructions.
  • They help organize related information.
